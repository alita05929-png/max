Create a Wallet

Download the MetaMask extension from the official website and install it in your browser. Fund it by transferring BNB from an exchange or another wallet.

Connect to DEX

Connect to a decentralized exchange (DEX) like Pancakeswap. Open the DEX website and click on "Connect Wallet".Then select MetaMask.

Swap BNB for $MAXSEASON

To swap BNB for MAX, select BNB in the "from" field and choose MAX in the "to" field using our contract address. Enter the amount of tokens you want & press swap.

Verify tokens

Verify your transaction status in MetaMask under the "Activity" tab or on Bscscan using your Ethereum address. You import the token to your MetaMask by pasting in the CA.
